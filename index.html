We are dedicated to the conservation of Kenya's diverse wildlife and the preservation of our natural heritage.

WHO WE ARE

KWS is a government agency under the Ministry of Tourism and Wildlife, responsible for the conservation and management of Kenya's wildlife and natural resources.
